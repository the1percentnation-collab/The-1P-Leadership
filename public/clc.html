How certification works

FOUR THINGS YOU HAVE TO DO.

The difference between a certification and a course is what it asks of you. These four requirements are locked before the first cohort starts and they do not move.

  • Complete all eight modules. Attendance alone does not carry you through.
  • Pass the written exam at 80 percent. Three attempts, drawn from a tagged question bank.
  • Submit one recorded coaching session reviewed against a published rubric. You see the rubric in module 8, so nothing about the review is a surprise.
  • Log 25 approved practice coaching hours. Real sessions with real people, logged in the portal and approved.

Plain words

WHAT THIS IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT.

WHAT IT IS

  • A proprietary certification in the A.L.I.G.N. framework. Built by The One Percent Nation, earned through modules, an exam, a reviewed session, and 25 logged hours.
  • An annual license to deliver that framework commercially, with client-facing products you are licensed to use with paying clients.
  • A system and a client base, for coaches who want both.

WHAT IT IS NOT

  • Not a state license. Life coaching is unregulated in all 50 states. No state issues a life coach license. The credential is a certification. The license is the right to use the framework.
  • Not ICF accredited. We do not imply otherwise anywhere, on this page or on the certificate.
  • Not a weekend certificate. You cannot finish without coaching real people.
When people ask about ICF, here is the answer we give and the answer you can give: this is a proprietary framework certification built for coaches who want a system and a client base, not a credential for corporate procurement.
