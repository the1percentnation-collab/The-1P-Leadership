Questions
Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from this page whenever you like. Your membership stays open until the end of the period you already paid for, and nothing renews after that.

What happens to courses I bought on their own?

They are yours, with or without a membership. The membership opens courses on top of what you own; it never takes anything away.

What is the founding rate?

The first 100 members pay $29 a month, or $290 a year, and keep that rate for as long as they stay members. After that the membership is $39 a month, or $390 a year.

Is annual billing worth it?

Annual is ten months for the price of twelve, billed once a year. It also counts as twelve paid months toward the certification credit.

Can I still buy a single course?

Yes. Every course is still sold on its own for people who want to own one outright.

When are the live sessions?

Once a month. The date and join link appear on the Events page and in your portal for members, and every session is recorded.
