About The One Percent

More than a brand. A movement.

The One Percent is a purpose-driven coaching and leadership development company built to redefine success. We help people and organizations who look successful on paper but feel misaligned break free from limiting beliefs and outdated measures of achievement. Our philosophy is simple: become one percent better every day.

Meet the Founder

The man behind
the movement.

Founder · Keynote Speaker · Author · Business & Life Coach

Anthony Brown Sr. built The One Percent Nation on a single conviction: success isn't a finish line. It's a discipline you build one percent at a time.

He didn't get here on an easy road. A high school dropout, Anthony had to figure it out the hard way, and the foundation was laid in the summers he spent working for his grandfather, who taught him the work ethic that made everything since possible.

As a keynote speaker, business coach, life coach, and author of I Can't: Is Not A Strategy, he has spent his career helping individuals and organizations break through limiting beliefs, realign with purpose, and turn intention into consistent action. His approach bridges mindset and execution, because who you are should match how you operate.

What began as a personal commitment to growth has become a movement: a community of people redefining what it means to win, and becoming one percent better every single day.

He does not just inspire a room. He gives people something to run the moment they walk out of it.

Why We Exist

Achievement
without alignment.

The One Percent was born from the realization that traditional definitions of success were incomplete. Too many people were chasing achievement while feeling disconnected from their purpose, overwhelmed by expectations, or caught in cycles of burnout and self-doubt.

We exist to challenge that story and set a new standard of success rooted in alignment, clarity, and growth. What began as a personal commitment to become one percent better each day became a framework for helping others do the same.

Small, consistent improvements grounded in purpose create lasting change.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is The One Percent Nation?
A coaching and leadership development company built on one principle: become one percent better every day. We work with organizations through keynotes, workshops, retreats, and 12-month programs, and with individuals through courses, live sessions, and a private member community.
Who is it for?
People and teams who are successful on paper but feel misaligned, stuck, or stretched thin in practice. That includes leaders, sales teams, entrepreneurs, high-potential professionals, and faith-based and nonprofit organizations.
How do you work with organizations?
Every engagement starts with a free 45-minute Alignment Audit. From there we build a keynote, workshop, retreat, or 12-month program around your team, drawing on our signature programs: The Mindset Reset, Purpose Pathways, and Leadership Ascension. See corporate programs.
What does membership include?
The Member Portal gives you the course library, your progress dashboard, live event replays, resource downloads, and the member community. You can sign in with email or Google. Become a member or sign in.
What is the 1P Certified Life Coach program?
A 16-week certification with live weekly coaching, a certification exam, and a reviewed coaching session. You finish with a credential, a license to the One Percent framework, and a client-ready program to deliver. Explore certification.
What is The Social Media Matrix?
A four-session mastermind for people who were handed the platforms and never handed the rules. You choose one platform on purpose, build a message people can repeat, set a weekly publishing rhythm that survives a busy month, and map thirty days of content before the final session ends. See the mastermind.
How do the live webinars work?
Live webinars cover framework deep dives, Q&A, and accountability check-ins. Registered attendees receive a replay link, and members get the full replay archive in the portal. Reserve your spot.
Where can I get I Can't?
On the Books page, where you can also read a free preview before you buy.
