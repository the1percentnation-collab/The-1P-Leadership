Questions

›Why can't I watch it all at once?

Because a six-week program you can finish in an afternoon changes nothing. A new week opens every seven days from the day you start. The rewrite happens at the speed of reps, not the speed of reading.

›When does the clock start?

The first time you open the course, not the moment you pay. Buy it as a gift, or start in January. Nothing burns while it sits.

›Do I need to have read the book?

No. The course stands on its own. It reads better alongside I CAN'T, which is why the bundle exists.

›Who sees my check-ins?

You. Each week ends with a private check-in stored under your own account. It is never posted to the community and never visible to other members.

›What happens after week six?

You unlock The Relapse Protocol, the bonus module for when the old sentence comes back. It will. You should already know what to run.
