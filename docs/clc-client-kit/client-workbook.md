# The A.L.I.G.N. Workbook

A six-week workbook for the A.L.I.G.N. program.

---

## Welcome

You are not here because something is broken. You are here because something is
out of line.

From the outside, most of your life looks correct. The work, the title, the
responsibilities, the people counting on you. On the inside there is a gap
between who you are and how you actually spend your days. You have probably been
managing that gap for a while. Working harder into it. Explaining it. Waiting
for a season to end so it closes on its own.

It does not close on its own. It closes when you see it clearly, take ownership
of your part, change the sentence you believe about yourself, aim at something
that fits the person you are becoming, and build a path you can walk on an
ordinary week.

That is A.L.I.G.N.

- **A. Awareness.** Seeing the belief clearly.
- **L. Leadership.** Taking ownership of your story.
- **I. Identity.** Rewriting who you believe you are.
- **G. Growth.** Choosing expansion over comfort.
- **N. Navigation.** Executing the path forward.

Six weeks. One stage at a time, in order. Your coach holds the structure and
asks the questions. You hold the pen. Every answer in this book is yours, and
nobody writes it for you.

---

## How to use this

**Write it down.** Do not do these exercises in your head. A belief loses most
of its power the moment it has to sit still on a page and be looked at. That
only happens in writing.

**Answer fast and honest before you answer well.** Your first answer is usually
the true one. The polished version arrives second and is often the one you have
been telling people for years.

**Do the between-session work.** The session is where you see it. The week is
where it changes. Fifteen to thirty minutes across the week is enough if it is
real.

**Keep every page.** The sentence you write in session one is the sentence you
rewrite in session three. The assessment you take before session one is the one
you retake in session six. This book is the record of the movement.

**One rule about honesty.** Nobody reads this but you and your coach. If you
write the version you would post publicly, you will get the results of a public
post.

**Missing a week is information, not failure.** If a commitment does not hold,
bring the data, not the apology. What broke and when is more useful than how
sorry you are.

**Before session one:** complete the Alignment Assessment and bring your scores.

## Keep going

Six weeks does not finish a life. It gives you a method you can run again on the
next thing.

When something is off, the order is always the same. See it clearly. Own your
part. Check the sentence you believe about yourself. Aim at something that fits
who you are becoming. Build a path that survives an ordinary week.

One percent better, on purpose, repeated. That is the whole thing.
