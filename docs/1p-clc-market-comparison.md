# 1P Certified Life Coach: Market Comparison

How the program stacks up against the certifications a buyer will compare it
to, and what has to be true for it to be worth $3,497. Checked September 2026
against the programs' own pages and current third-party reviews. Prices move;
re-check before quoting any of them in copy. No em dashes.

ICF ACC itself, for reference: 60 education hours, 100 coaching hours (75
paid), 10 mentor coaching hours, a performance evaluation, and an exam.

## Where 1P-CLC holds up

- **Rigor above its price band.** Exam, reviewed recording, and 25 logged hours
  put it structurally next to Coach Training Alliance and Jay Shetty, not next
  to the $200 to $500 video certificates. Most non-accredited programs at any
  price skip the reviewed recording entirely.
- **Live delivery.** Twelve weekly live calls is more live contact than Jay
  Shetty's 12 total live hours and comparable to CTA. Buyers pay for access to
  the founder; this is where a small program beats a large one.
- **A product to sell on day one.** The six-week A.L.I.G.N. client program,
  workbook, and Alignment Assessment are what iPEC, CTI, and Lumia do not
  hand you. They teach you to coach and leave you to invent an offer. This is
  the strongest line on the sales page and the reason the license renews.
- **Price sits where the spec intended.** Below every credible comparable,
  above every certificate mill. Do not move it.

## Where it was thin, and what changed in this build

1. **Observed practice before the graded recording.** Every major program puts
   feedback on a student's coaching before anything is scored: CTA group
   mentoring, Lumia's 10 mentor hours with written observation feedback, iPEC's
   live modules, CTI's 100 hours. The spec had one graded recording at week 13
   and nothing observed before it. That produces a high capstone return rate
   and shaky graduates. **Now built in:** fixed peer triads from module 1
   (coach, client, observer rotating weekly, observer gives feedback against
   the four rubric criteria), plus two feedback-only recordings after modules
   4 and 6. The graded capstone is never a student's first reviewed session.
2. **Practice hours that are all classmates.** 25 hours is a fair entry bar
   (Jay Shetty asks 20, Lumia 50, ICF 100), but if all 25 come from peers the
   graduate has never coached a stranger. **Now built in:** modules 4 onward
   require a growing share of hours with clients who are not classmates.
3. **Written rubric published in advance.** Done in module 8 and
   `docs/1p-clc-rubric.md`, with what a 2, a 3, and a 5 sound like.

## Still open, and worth deciding before the founding cohort

- **A code of ethics students sign.** Every program above has one. One page,
  signed at enrollment, covers confidentiality, scope, referral, dual
  relationships, and marketing claims. Cheap, and it is what the license
  revocation clause rests on.
- **Refund and withdrawal terms.** Nothing exists yet. The sales page says
  "Terms are shown at checkout"; Stripe needs actual terms to show.
- **CE content for renewal.** Renewal requires 10 CE credits and there is no CE
  content. Four quarterly live masterclasses, recorded, would cover it and cost
  Anthony about eight hours a year.
- **Delivery bench.** One founder running 12 live calls, 40 feedback-only
  reviews, and 20 graded reviews is fine for cohort one and not for cohort
  three. The Master Trainer License exists for this; plan to certify one or
  two reviewers out of the founding cohort.
- **Accreditation, later.** Not ICF is the right call now: the buyer here wants
  a system and clients, and ICF matters mostly to corporate procurement. The
  curriculum already clears ICF's 60-hour threshold, so Level 1 accreditation
  stays a real option once a cohort has finished and there is evidence of
  demand. Say nothing about it in copy until then.

## Verdict

At $3,497 with the observed-practice changes, the program is a fair trade: a
graduate leaves with more live instruction than Jay Shetty, a published
rubric and reviewed recording that most programs at this price skip, and a
client offer none of them provide. At $1,997 for the founding cohort it is the
best value in the table. Without the observed-practice pieces it would have
been a strong course with a certificate attached, and buyers who compare
carefully would have seen that.
