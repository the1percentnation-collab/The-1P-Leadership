# 1P Certified Life Coach: Program Spec

Context file for build work. Source of truth for the certification product.
Brand voice rules live in the one-percent-context skill. No em dashes.

---

## 1. What this is

A proprietary certification in the A.L.I.G.N. framework, plus an annual license
to use the framework and 1P marks commercially.

**Not a license.** Life coaching is unregulated in all 50 states. No state issues
a life coach license. Never use the word "license" to describe the credential
itself. The credential is a certification. The license is the IP usage right,
renewed annually.

**Not ICF accredited.** Do not imply otherwise anywhere in copy, UI, or
certificate assets. Standing answer when asked: this is a proprietary framework
certification built for coaches who want a system and a client base, not a
credential for corporate procurement.

Two products, connected:

- **1P Certified Life Coach (1P-CLC)**. The credential. Earned once.
- **A.L.I.G.N. Practitioner License**. The right to deliver the framework
  commercially. Renewed annually, revocable if standards lapse.

---

## 2. Priority and sequencing

Life track first. Inbound demand is for life coach certification, not the
leadership version. Nothing is built yet, so there is no sunk cost to protect.

Build as a single linear course. Do NOT architect the two-track (Life /
Leadership) split in v1. That is a v2 concern once the founding cohort is
through.

Sell the founding cohort before the course is finished. Enrollment closes on a
fixed date, module one drops two weeks later, delivery is live and weekly.
Recordings become the evergreen asset. Module 6 does not need to exist when
enrollment opens.

**Minimum viable build before launch:**
1. Full module outline
2. Module 1 materials
3. Certification requirements, written and locked
4. Sales page
5. Hour-logging in the portal (see section 6)

## 3. Program structure

**16-week cohort. 65 to 70 total hours. 4 to 5 hours per week.**

- Weeks 1 to 12: eight modules, live weekly call on a fixed day
- Weeks 13 to 16: practicum, exam, recorded session review, certification

Market it as a single fixed number ("Certified in 16 weeks"), never a range.
Ranges make buyers default to the long end and hesitate.

**12-week fast track** for existing coaches and professional leaders. Same
requirements, compressed cadence, identical price. No discount.

### Benchmarks this was set against

| Program | Length | Hours | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coach Training Alliance | ~6 months | ~60 total | $3,497 |
| Coach U Core Essentials | 6 days to 15 months | 77 | varies |
| CTI (Co-Active) | varies | 100+ | ~$14,500 |
| Coach Training EDU | ~6 months | Level 2 | mid |
| Lumia | ~9 months | Level 1/2 | $5,495+ |
| iPEC | 7 to 10 months | ~320 | $9,995 to $11,950 |

CTA is the closest structural comparable and validates 60 hours over 6 months at
$3,497.

## 4. Certification requirements

Lock these before the first cohort. Retrofitting rigor onto sold students is the
one genuinely hard-to-undo mistake.

- Completion of all eight modules
- Written exam
- One recorded coaching session, reviewed against a published rubric
- Minimum 25 logged practice coaching hours

Do not compress these to shorten the program. Compress lecture time and push into
application instead. These four items are the entire difference between a
certification and a course.

## 5. Pricing

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Founding cohort | $1,997 | 20 seats, publicly capped, never repeated |
| 1P-CLC standard | $3,497 | Includes year one license |
| Certified + Practice Build | $5,997 | Adds acquisition system, 6mo mentorship, observed coaching |
| Master Trainer License | $9,500 | Requires 1P-CLC first |
| Annual license renewal | $597 | Requires logged hours + CE |
| Second track add-on (v2) | $797 | Pure margin, asset already exists |

Payment plans: $697 x 6 or $397 x 10. Expect 60 to 70 percent plan uptake, so
Stripe subscriptions are required, not just one-time Checkout.

Positioning rationale: the market splits between $197 to $500 certificates and
the ICF ACC floor of roughly $3,400. $3,497 sits above every non-accredited
certificate and just under the ICF floor. Never discount the price to compete
with $497 programs. The moment you do, you have invited the comparison.

## 6. Portal requirements (build these in v1)

- **Coaching hour log.** Gated. Renewal button does not activate without minimum
  hours plus CE credits. Ship this before the first cohort starts.
- **Stripe subscriptions** for payment plans and annual renewal.
- **Public coach directory.** Listing is a renewal benefit, which makes renewal
  feel like an asset instead of a fee. Route inbound 1P leads to listed coaches.
- **Certificate generation** with track designation and license expiry date.
- **Rubric + recorded session upload** for the review requirement.

## 7. Licensing model

Two reference models researched:

- **Maxwell Leadership.** Certified members deliver Maxwell curriculum, manuals,
  scripts, workbooks, PowerPoints and facilitator guides, and keep 100 percent of
  what they earn. Recent offers include lifetime access with no renewal fee.
  Historically $4,000 to $6,000. 40,000+ members, 150+ countries.
- **Proctor Gallagher Institute.** Consultants deliver PGI material without
  creating their own product. More controlled. 86 countries.

**Go with the controlled model.** License a defined set of A.L.I.G.N.
client-facing products, with an annual license fee and a revenue share on
anything sold under the 1P name. Maxwell can give away the back end because
volume and brand equity carry it. 1P cannot yet, and the revenue share is what
makes each certified coach an ongoing asset rather than a one-time sale.

**Build before enrollment opens:** the client-facing resale products. A workbook,
an assessment, and a six-week client program. This is what turns the
certification from training into a business in a box and it is the strongest
element on the sales page.

---

## 8. Revenue logic

The certification is the acquisition event. The license renewal is the business.
100 active coaches at $597 is roughly $60K recurring with near-zero delivery
cost. Price the certification to acquire. Price the renewal to compound.

---

## 9. Sales page core copy

> Certified in 16 weeks. Four to five hours per week. Live weekly coaching, a
> certification exam, and a reviewed coaching session. You finish with a
> credential, a framework license, and a client-ready program to sell.

Lead with what it is, not with what it is not. The audience wants permission and
a path, not ICF accreditation.
